Analysis of your thoughts

For each automatic thought (AT): rate how much you believe it, pick any distortions, then talk back to it. Imagine what you would say to a friend who said the same thing. Ask yourself, “What is the evidence for the reasonable response?”

Results

Go back to each thought and re-rate how much you believe it, then re-rate the intensity of your feeling. Even a slight drop is a win.
